AEGIS // DRONE DOME

A simulated swarm of mini drones holds slots on a hemispherical shell around an asset. When a radar track is classified as hostile, the best-placed drone leaves the shell to capture it, and the rest of the shell re-spaces itself to close the gap.

Pipeline (every 50 ms tick)

  1. Sense: radar reports with range-dependent noise and missed scans
  2. Track: IMM filter per track with Hungarian association
  3. Assess: hostile if the projected path enters the shell
  4. Assign: Hungarian drone-to-threat assignment that minimizes time to intercept
  5. Guide: lead pursuit to the predicted intercept point
  6. Heal: Fibonacci-lattice re-layout of the remaining shell
